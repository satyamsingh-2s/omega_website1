# Omega Website --- Funding Section Update

## 1. Purpose

Replace the existing pricing / reward section with a **Funding & Journey
section**.

The section must NOT look like: - a SaaS pricing table - a crowdfunding
checkout - a donation request - a desperate appeal for financial support

It should feel like a natural continuation of the Omega story:

> **This is what Omega is becoming. If you believe in it, you can join
> the journey.**

The section is an invitation to participate through funding,
sponsorship, collaboration, or another meaningful contribution.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Core Design Principle

The section should communicate:

> **Invitation, not desperation.**

Omega is being built independently and has a clear direction.

The visitor should feel:

-   Omega already has a vision.
-   Omega is actively being built.
-   The project values independence and craftsmanship.
-   There are different ways to participate.
-   Financial support is one option, not the entire purpose of the
    section.
-   Contact is the beginning of a conversation, not a checkout flow.

Do NOT visually or verbally imply urgency, financial difficulty, or
dependency on the visitor.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3. Existing Section to Remove

Completely remove the current three pricing cards:

-   \$15 --- The Offline Pass
-   \$45 --- The Founding Companion
-   \$120 --- The Journey Patron

Also remove:

-   "Most Chosen"
-   "Choose \$15"
-   "Choose \$45"
-   "Choose \$120"
-   reward / tier language
-   pricing-card interactions
-   payment-oriented visual hierarchy

Do not replace the cards with different monetary tiers.

There should be **no pricing table** in this section.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4. Final Content

## Section Label

``` text
FUND THE FUTURE OF OMEGA
```

This is the only place in the section where the word **FUND** should
appear.

Do not repeatedly use "fund", "funding", "funded", or similar language
throughout the section.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Main Heading

``` text
Join the journey of Omega.
```

This is the primary headline.

It should be large, editorial, and visually dominant.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Supporting Copy

Use exactly this content:

``` text
Omega is being built independently, with craftsmanship and user sovereignty at the center.

It is a long-term project — shaped through ideas, experiments, engineering, and the people who choose to follow its journey.

There are many ways to become part of what comes next.
```

Do not add unnecessary marketing language.

Do not use phrases such as: - "We need your help" - "Every contribution
matters" - "Support our dream" - "We can't do this without you" -
"Donate now" - "Back Omega" - "Please consider supporting us"

The tone must remain confident, calm, independent, and intentional.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 5. Participation Options

Introduce a small subsection:

``` text
WAYS TO JOIN
```

Then present three participation paths.

## FUND

``` text
Help Omega grow faster.
```

## SPONSOR

``` text
Support a specific part of the journey.
```

## COLLABORATE

``` text
Bring an idea, skill, or perspective to Omega.
```

These are **not cards representing products or plans**.

They are simple editorial information blocks.

No prices.

No buttons inside each block.

No payment UI.

No badges.

No "recommended" option.

No hierarchy suggesting that one method is more valuable than another.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 6. Primary CTA

Use one primary CTA at the bottom:

``` text
JOIN THE JOURNEY →
```

The CTA should lead to the existing contact mechanism / contact section.

It should NOT open a payment or checkout flow directly.

The intended interaction is:

``` text
JOIN THE JOURNEY
        ↓
Contact
        ↓
Conversation about funding / sponsorship / collaboration / other contribution
```

This keeps the section human and flexible.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Use generous whitespace.

The section should feel closer to the site's large editorial sections
than to a conventional landing-page CTA.

Recommended structure:

``` text

    ↓
Large top spacing
    ↓
Section label
    ↓
Large headline
    ↓
Supporting copy
    ↓
Generous separation
    ↓
WAYS TO JOIN
    ↓
Three participation columns
    ↓
Generous separation
    ↓
Primary CTA
    ↓

```

Do not compress the content just to reduce section height.

Whitespace is an intentional part of the Omega visual language.

# 12. Animation Direction

The animation must support the **story of Omega**.

Do not add generic website animation simply because animation is
possible.

The section's animation should communicate:

> **Omega is moving forward, and the visitor can choose to become part
> of that journey.**

Use restrained motion.

Recommended sequence:

### On section entry

1.  Section label appears with a subtle upward fade.
2.  Main headline follows with a slightly slower reveal.
3.  Supporting paragraphs appear sequentially with a small stagger.
4.  "WAYS TO JOIN" appears.
5.  FUND / SPONSOR / COLLABORATE appear sequentially or with a very
    subtle grouped reveal.
6.  CTA appears last.

The CTA being last gives the content a natural narrative:

``` text
What Omega is
        ↓
Where it is going
        ↓
How someone can participate
        ↓
Join the journey
```

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 13. Animation Constraints

Do NOT use:

-   cursor trails
-   mouse-following effects
-   continuous particle systems
-   heavy WebGL
-   canvas-based background animation
-   expensive blur animations
-   constantly moving gradients
-   infinite decorative loops
-   large parallax systems
-   animation that runs continuously after the section is visible

The website should remain lightweight.

Animation should be primarily:

``` text
scroll-triggered
+
short-lived
+
low-cost
```

After the reveal animation finishes, the section should remain visually
stable.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 14. Optional Micro-interaction

Participation options may have a very subtle hover interaction.

Example:

``` text
FUND
```

On hover:

-   slight text movement
-   subtle orange accent
-   small underline / border transition

Keep the movement extremely small.

The interaction should feel like an editorial interface, not a flashy
SaaS landing page.

## Desktop

Use three columns:

``` text
FUND        SPONSOR        COLLABORATE
```

Keep them aligned and evenly distributed.

## Tablet

Allow the columns to remain horizontal if there is enough width.

Otherwise reduce spacing before changing the structure.

## Mobile

Stack vertically:

``` text
WAYS TO JOIN

FUND
Help make continued development possible.

SPONSOR
Support a specific part of the journey.

COLLABORATE
Bring an idea, skill, or perspective to Omega.

JOIN THE JOURNEY →
```

Maintain generous vertical spacing.

Do not create oversized cards on mobile.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 16. Accessibility

Maintain:

-   semantic heading hierarchy
-   readable contrast
-   keyboard-accessible CTA
-   visible focus state
-   reduced-motion support

If the user has `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, disable or
significantly reduce the reveal animation.

The content must remain fully understandable without animation.

# 20. Design Intent

The final section should leave the visitor with this feeling:

> **Omega is independent. Omega has a direction. I can choose to be part
> of it.**

It should feel like an invitation to a long-term journey, not a sales
pitch.

The section should visually belong to the same world as:

-   The Knowledge Tree
-   Active Flow
-   Desk Omega
-   Memory / Revision Notes
-   Progress / Footprints
-   The Future
-   Local-first philosophy

The final message is not:

> "Give Omega money."

It is:

> **"Omega is being built. If you believe in where it is going, join the
> journey."**
